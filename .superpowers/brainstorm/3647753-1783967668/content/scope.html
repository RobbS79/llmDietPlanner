How much price surface do we build?

The backend already ships an honest per-recipe range (low–high, per-portion, a confidence flag). The question is how much of it we show. Numbers below are illustrative. Pick the shape that feels right — click to select.

Priced shopping list · recommended

The ingredient list becomes a real per-recipe "nákupní seznam": each priced line shows its cost, unpriced lines are marked honestly, and a from–to total + coverage sit at the bottom. Needs a small backend change (expose per-line cost + priced/total counts).

Everything in B, plus the plan/dashboard sums the recipe ranges into a weekly range. Matches the landing's "weekly receipt / 1 247 Kč" hero — but summing ranges compounds imprecision, and coverage gaps make a headline total easy to distrust.

My lean: B — it's what the landing actually needs (a truthful, itemized price picture per recipe), it stays honest about coverage, and it dodges the compounding-error trap of a single weekly headline number. We can still add a soft plan-level range later if you want C.
