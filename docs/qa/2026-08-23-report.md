## Failure detail — garbled public ingredient lists (blocker)

- Affected pages (all public, indexed, top of the /recepty grid):
  - https://eatalnicek.eu/recepty/70/tofu-s-ryzi-a-cuketou/
  - https://eatalnicek.eu/recepty/71/ovesna-kase-s-bananem/
  - https://eatalnicek.eu/recepty/72/rychle-tofu-se-spenatem-a-ryzi/
- Symptom: ingredient list shows raw catalog product names with internal IDs, e.g. `pappudia tofu (#2153)`, and no quantities/units. Present in the rendered page, SSR HTML, and `recipeIngredient` JSON-LD structured data.
- Likely origin: these three Recipe-showcase rows were created/re-synced with catalog-annotated ingredient strings (consistent with the recent obtainability/ingredient-rewrite work) instead of display-form lines.
- A real user landing on the newest recipes immediately sees broken content → NO-GO.
