## New findings (not regressions)

**F1 — `max_time_minutes` is extracted but not enforced on swap candidates.**
The prompt says *max 30 minut*, and the facet extractor captured
`max_time_minutes: 30` correctly. The swap candidates offered were 35 min,
35 min, 25 min on turn 1 and 9 min, **45 min**, **90 min** on turn 2. The 90-min
*Kynuté Honzovy buchty* is triple the stated ceiling. This is hard evidence for
the "candidates ignore stated intent" item — the facet is present and correct,
so the gap is in candidate ranking/filtering, not extraction.

**F2 — swap cards show whole-recipe kcal as if per portion.**
Candidate cards displayed 1802 kcal, 3338 kcal and 3080 kcal for a single
breakfast, against a plan averaging 1945 kcal/day. This is the known corpus
nutrition-basis defect surfacing in a very visible place: the card a user reads
right before choosing a swap. The plan's own meal cards are correct (605 kcal
for Ovesná kaše), so the wrong basis is being read on the candidate path
specifically.

Both are user-visible; F2 is the more damaging of the two.
